# Hooks that work for @uk_speaks.ai

Score every hook: `./reel score "your hook text"` (engine/hookscore.py, five heuristics). Target ≥ 90. It catches weak hooks; it doesn't predict views, so pick among the strong ones by fit with what he actually says.

What scored ≥ 90 and he approved:
- "Stop making the SAME AI SLIDES. Fix it free in 3 steps" (94.8)
- "Stop losing your ChatGPT memory. Move it to Claude free" (100)
- "Stop paying for posters. Copy any design free in 2 steps" (94.8)
- "Stop. This viral ChatGPT prompt only works half. I tested it" (94.8)

Patterns: start with "Stop" (or a 1-word imperative), name the thing the viewer already does, add a concrete payoff (number of steps, free, minutes), use tension words (only, free, half). 5–12 words. Must match his first spoken sentence; never promise what the video doesn't show.

For the panel template the hook is 3 lines: small / BIG RED (2–3 words, the pain) / small (the payoff). Always offer 4–6 options with scores and let him pick (AskUserQuestion). Flag any option that implies something he didn't say.
