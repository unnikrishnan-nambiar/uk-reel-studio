# Content ideas and playbook

Files in `ideas/`:
- `outliers-2026-11-01.md` — proven outlier reels in his niche (views vs creator median, why they worked).
- `7-day-hype-test-plan.md` — "Hype Test" series (Sathyamo, Hype-o?): test a viral AI claim live, verdict SATHYAM/HYPE, PM angle, keyword CTA. Hooks scored ≥ 90.
- `reel-script-*.md` — ready scripts (POSTER, MEMORY, HONEST, JOBS), `reel-scripts-2026-10-30.md`, `viral-ai-reels-2026-10-27.md`, `animate-prompts.md`.

Playbook he follows:
1. Find 3 outliers in the topic (far above the creator's median; ignore boosted ones with tiny likes).
2. Write his own version + one thing only he adds (the product-manager angle).
3. Record 3 hooks, post as trial reels.
4. Auto-DM the keyword (CTA "Comment KEYWORD") → free guide page behind it (skill guide-page).

What his audience responds to: real demos (show the result first), raw "me at the laptop" footage, simple English-first on-screen text, Malayalam/Manglish voice, a share line ("send this to someone who…"). Comments run 3–5× likes on his best reels, so keyword CTAs work.

Never invent stats or claims; check current facts (prices, plan limits) on the web before scripting.
