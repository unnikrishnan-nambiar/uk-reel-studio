# 7 days, 7 reels: "Hype Test" series (uk_speaks.ai)

**Same technique every day:**
1. Take a viral AI claim that already got 1M+ views (an outlier).
2. Show the claim for 2 seconds: their cover or a quote on screen, with credit (@handle). Don't re-upload their video.
3. **Test it live, raw:** you at your laptop or phone, a real screen, real waiting, real mistakes. Your audience loves demos.
4. **Verdict:** ☐ SATHYAM (fact) / ☐ HYPE, ticked on screen, plus one honest line on what didn't work.
5. **The part only you add:** the PM / "what this means at work" angle.
6. CTA keyword, then a guide page with the working prompt and steps.
7. Post all 3 hooks as trial reels.

**Rule for every hook:** the verdict in the hook ("only works half", "only 2 work") must match what you actually find. Record the hooks **after** the test, and swap the words if the result is different.
