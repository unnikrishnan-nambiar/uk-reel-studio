# uk_speaks.ai: 9 reel scripts (30 Oct 2026)

**How to record each reel:** record all 3 hooks (one take each), then the body once. The body is written to work after any of the 3 hooks. I'll cut 3 versions for Trial Reels.

- **Hook scores** come from hookscore.py (length, specificity, stakes, front-loading, speaking to the viewer). It's a heuristic: it catches weak hooks well but can't predict a winner between strong ones. The Trial Reels are the real test.
- **Virality score** is my estimate out of 100: mass appeal 30, everyday usefulness 25, shareability 20, easy to film 15, low risk 10.
- **On-screen hook** is in English. **Spoken hook** is in Manglish, and uses "stop", never "nirthu".
- Every body ends: keyword CTA, then "Follow cheyyu, baaki namukku AI vechu nokkaam."
